The "Why" Behind the Design

The Problem

Estate Agents were losing commission because leads were being lost in email inboxes. Financial Advisors were frustrated by receiving incomplete data. The relationship was breaking down due to poor process.

The Solution

By building a shared "Truth Source," we aligned the incentives. Agents got visibility on their pipeline, and Advisors got high-quality pre-qualified data. The UI was deliberately stripped back to resemble consumer apps to ensure adoption by non-technical users.
